# Akhil Gujarat CMS User Guide

## Logging In
1. Navigate to `/admin/login`.
2. Enter your email and password.
3. If successful, you will be redirected to the Dashboard.

## Managing Articles
- **Create**: Go to "સમાચાર (Articles)" -> "નવો લેખ (New Article)".
- **Edit**: Click the edit icon next to an article in the list.
- **Drafts**: You can save articles as drafts. They will not appear on the public site until their status is changed to `published`.
- **Images**: Upload cover images directly. Accepted formats: `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`, `.webp`, `.gif`.

## E-Paper
- Upload PDF versions of the daily e-paper in the "ઈ-પેપર (E-Paper)" section.
- You can specify the date for each e-paper.

## Categories & Cities
- Manage news categories (e.g., Gujarat, National, Sports) in the "વિભાગો (Categories)" section.
- Manage cities for localized news in the "શહેરો (Cities)" section.

## Advertisements
- Manage banner ads in the "જાહેરાતો (Ads)" section.
- You can assign ads to specific slots (e.g., `home_top`, `article_sidebar`).
- Set Start Date and End Date to schedule ads automatically.

## Security & Sessions
- Your session will automatically expire after 24 hours. A warning will appear 15 minutes before expiration.
- Save your drafts frequently!
